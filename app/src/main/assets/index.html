Battery Performance

Starting/ending voltage, lowest observed voltage and voltage sag under load help track pack performance over time.

Battery current is not proven available from the SR48400. Motor amperage cannot be used to calculate accurate amp-hours or battery health percentage.

Repeat similar trips and compare sag at similar current and temperature to watch for deterioration. A resting voltage check and proper capacity test are still valuable.

Battery Performance Log

Battery samples are saved automatically while USB is connected, even without an active trip.

Voltage dips under acceleration can reveal sag, but motor output current is not battery current. Amp-hours, true battery capacity and exact miles remaining cannot be calculated from these readings.

Record a Rested Voltage Check

After the batteries sit for at least 2 hours without charging or driving, enter your measured total pack voltage. For best accuracy, check after a longer rest, away from recent charging surface charge.

Estimates assume eight 6V flooded lead-acid batteries in series, near room temperature. Voltage estimates are approximate and cannot detect a weak individual battery.
